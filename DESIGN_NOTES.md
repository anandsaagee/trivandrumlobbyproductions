## Design Reference Review

### Key Patterns Observed

**Hero Treatment**
- A24 uses featured content blocks with poster imagery, title, and action buttons (Buy Tickets, Get Updates)
- Visual Ronin (cinematographer portfolio) leads with strong hero imagery and minimal text overlay
- Pattern: Large format imagery is the primary visual anchor, not typography

**Project Grid and Portfolio**
- A24 displays films as card-based grids with poster, title, year, and contextual call-to-action
- Cards are clickable and link to detail pages with more information
- Grid repeats sections to emphasize content priority
- Pattern: Poster-first, minimal card text, clean grid that scales responsively

**Navigation**
- A24 uses horizontal primary navigation with secondary menu (Jobs, Press, App)
- Simple, uncluttered approach with clear hierarchy
- Pattern: 4-5 main items, supplementary links in secondary menu or footer

**Credit Presentation**
- Wayfarer Films (redirected to Visual Ronin) shows individual project pages with full credits and collaborator links
- Pattern: Credits appear on project detail pages, not homepage

**Contact Approach**
- A24 uses email signup and social media links, no traditional contact form on homepage
- Social media integration (Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook) is prominent in footer
- Pattern: Multiple low-friction contact points (email, social) rather than single form

**Team Presentation**
- Visual Ronin includes biography section with professional philosophy and principles
- Brief, credibility-focused copy focused on the person's background and values
- Pattern: Personal branding combined with professional credentials

**Mobile Behavior**
- Responsive card grids that adapt to screen size
- Expandable/hamburger menu for mobile navigation
- Pattern: Content-first, navigation collapses, images remain primary focus

### What Works Well
- Poster and imagery-led design creates immediate cinematic credibility
- Simple, uncluttered navigation reduces cognitive load
- Clear separation between homepage and project detail pages
- Multiple contact methods accommodate different user preferences
- Grid layouts scale naturally from 1-6+ items

### What to Avoid
- Over-decoration or excessive animation (minimize motion, respect prefers-reduced-motion)
- Gradients, neon colors, or busy backgrounds (red and white only, clean palette)
- Cluttered footer with too many links (curate essential links only)
- Invisible or tiny contact information (make it discoverable)
- Long-form copy on homepage (save depth for project pages and about section)

### Trivandrum Lobby Specific Notes
- Small production house: emphasize real work and credibility over scale
- Focus on two completed projects and one upcoming: use grid that naturally expands later
- Team section should be honest and concise (2-6 cards max)
- Contact should offer clear paths (business/partnerships and general enquiries)
- Instagram presence: feed grid with links to real posts, not auto-embedded
